PRIVACY

What we collect.

Short version: just enough to count how many people use V-Client. No accounts, no names, no IP addresses.

In the launcher

  • Once per day, one anonymous launch event: a random ID generated on your PC when you first run the launcher, the launcher version, and the platform (Windows).
  • The random ID is not linked to your Minecraft name, Microsoft or Ely.by account, or your PC. Deleting %APPDATA%\V-Client\settings.json creates a new one.
  • The launcher also counts how many different Minecraft accounts have played, using a one-way hash of the account ID. Your player name is never sent.
  • We do not store IP addresses or browser/PC details. The hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep short-lived technical logs for security, as any host does.
  • Your game settings, sign-ins and files stay on your PC.

On this website

  • Page views and download-button clicks are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • The download counter reads public download numbers from GitHub. Your browser remembers the number for 10 minutes so it is not requested again.
  • The installer is downloaded from GitHub Releases, so GitHub sees that download under its own privacy policy.
